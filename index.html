GOOD JUDGMENT IS GEAR

Prepare well.
Come home well.

Mountains are powerful environments. No checklist removes all risk, but thoughtful preparation helps you make better decisions.

More learning resources ↗
01

Plan the route

Match the objective to your team's ability, check access rules, and leave an itinerary with someone reliable.

02

Read the conditions

Check forecasts, daylight, avalanche information, and local reports. Set clear turnaround criteria.

03

Carry the essentials

Bring suitable layers, navigation, water, food, first aid, lighting, and activity-specific safety equipment.

04

Know when to turn back

Fatigue, uncertainty, poor weather, or changing conditions are valid reasons to stop or change plans.
